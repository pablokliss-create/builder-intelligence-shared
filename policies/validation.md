# Validation Policy

Evidence priority:
1. Result from a real project.
2. Controlled reproducible test.
3. Official docs/changelog/release notes.
4. Source code, issues, PRs, discussions.
5. Strong methodology/research.
6. Multiple independent community reports.
7. Isolated opinion.
8. Promotional material.

Before changing a recommendation, record what changed, when it was checked, why the status changed, and whether migration is actually justified for existing projects.
